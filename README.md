## Things I'm Building

### [Ungated](https://useungated.com)
A job search tracker I designed and built end to end, written from a hospital bed during a month-long fight with sepsis. Drag applications from prospect to offer, keep contacts, salary, and next steps in one place, and share your board with a coach or recruiter. It works offline, keeps an unemployment work-search log that follows state rules, and uses passwordless sign-in. No subscription, no saved credit card.
